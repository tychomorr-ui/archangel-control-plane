# ARCHANGEL Living Control Room Specification

## Experience Thesis

ARCHANGEL should feel like an attentive sovereign intelligence—not through noisy decoration, but through **layered perception**. Its visual system presents a quiet cosmic field, an active central aperture, legible signals, and evidence-bearing relationships. The interface earns its sense of life from real state transitions: stale proof, verified receipt, blocked policy, pending human decision, declared inventory change, and verified outcome.

## Information Hierarchy

| Layer | Operator question | Visual behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **Attention horizon** | What needs me now? | A compact prioritized ribbon for stale proof, policy blockers, approvals, and unknown inventory. |
| **Aperture core** | What is ARCHANGEL doing? | A living central geometry that reflects the current authority mode: observation, review, controlled dispatch, or verification. |
| **Truth lenses** | Which truth am I looking at? | Switchable, persistent layers: physical inventory, logical roles, evidence, intent, and policy. |
| **Ecosystem field** | What exists and what is related? | A graph-like constellation with clear declared, observed, verified, and unplaced states. |
| **Evidence rail** | Why should I trust this? | Receipts, freshness, audits, scope, approvals, and policy explanations available without leaving context. |

## Signature Components

| Component | Job | Interaction contract |
|---|---|---|
| **Aether Aperture** | A concentric ARCHANGEL core that signals the current control mode. | It is always labeled; animation stops for reduced-motion preferences; it never represents a real network connection by itself. |
| **Truth Lens Bar** | Selects physical, logical, evidence, policy, or intent perspective. | A lens changes presentation only; all raw state labels remain visible to assistive technology. |
| **Signal Horizon** | Groups actionable attention into a short temporal queue. | Every signal has a source, freshness/authority state, and link to supporting evidence. |
| **Intent Chamber** | Lets the operator draft a bounded plain-language command and see its normalized form. | It cannot hide a command, bypass policy, or offer an arbitrary terminal. |
| **Evidence Thread** | Connects node, request, policy result, receipt, and outcome. | A missing receipt is displayed as missing; no inferred success state is allowed. |
| **Placement Ledger** | Holds future location recommendations separately from physical inventory. | A recommended region is never shown as a host location. |

## Visual Direction

The environment combines obsidian space, cyan ion light, ultraviolet analysis, amber declaration, emerald verification, and rare rose denial. Panels are not flat cards; they are translucent “slabs” with a faint internal grid, edge glow, and a procedural energy field. Typography uses display text for decisions and monospaced microcopy for source/state labels.

Motion is constrained to transforms and opacity. It has a purpose: a verified receipt resolves a signal, a pending intent slowly breathes, and a focus transition aligns the aperture with the selected lens. Continuous effects remain low-contrast and pause under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Responsive and Accessible Behavior

On narrow screens, the control room collapses to a single evidence-first sequence: attention horizon, aperture, active lens, and contextual evidence. All lens controls, command actions, and graph-inspector links remain keyboard reachable. Color never carries state alone; every mode uses text, iconography, and an explicit source label.

## Product Boundaries

The living interface does not create hidden autonomy. ARCHANGEL will continue to distinguish **declared**, **observed**, **verified**, **stale**, **blocked**, and **unplaced**. It must not render a transit line as active traffic, a map dot as an installed agent, or a pulsating core as a command executing without an auditable event.
